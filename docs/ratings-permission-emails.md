## Parfumo

**Subject:** Showing Parfumo ratings, notes and bottle pictures on a collector site — request for your OK

Hello Parfumo team,

I run **Eau de Fragrances** (eaudezhivko.vercel.app, soon eaudefragrances.com), a free site where fragrance collectors keep track of the bottles they own and the ones they want.

When a member adds the Parfumo link of a perfume, the card on our site shows that perfume's Parfumo rating, its note pyramid and its bottle picture, clearly credited to Parfumo and linking back to the perfume's page on Parfumo. Our home page also has a small carousel of eight well-known bottles, each credited and linking to its Parfumo page. We read each page only when a link is added and at most once a week after that, with our own identifiable User-Agent. We don't copy or store the pictures: they load from media.parfumo.com, which uses some of your bandwidth. We don't copy reviews.

We'd like to make sure you're happy with this, the bottle pictures in particular. Is there an official API or data feed we should use instead, and are there attribution or display rules you'd like us to follow? We'll gladly adjust or stop if you prefer.
